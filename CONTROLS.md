# Controls

*i've realized this isn't mentioned at all in the game, so here it is!*


Drive
- Translation (field relative)
    - WASD or Left Joystick (full)
- Rotation
    - J/L or Right Joystick (left and right)
- Auto Algining
    - Q/E or Left/Right Bummper (left/right align)

Intake
- Drop Intake
    - Shift or Left Trigger
- Transfer (intake -> arm)
    - 1 or Pov Up
- Summon Coral
    - Tab or Pov Left

Scoring
- Elevator
    - L4: P or Controller Y
    - L3: O or Controller X
    - L2: I or Controller B
    - L1: U or Controller A (not implemented)
- Score
    - K or Right Trigger
